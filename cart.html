Cart

Choose how the cart opens in Theme settings › Cart.

Cart type

OptionBehavior
Drawer (default)Adding a product opens a side drawer. Shoppers can change quantities, remove items and check out without leaving the page.
PageAdding a product updates the cart count. The cart icon goes to the /cart page.

With Collection to show when cart is empty, an empty cart suggests products from that collection.

Cart page settings

  • Show order notes: shoppers can leave a note, for example a gift message. Notes save automatically and appear on the order in your admin.
  • Summary color scheme: the color scheme of the totals panel.

What the cart shows

  • The product image, title and options, with custom line item properties.
  • Subscription plans.
  • Unit prices, for example "€4.20 / 100 ml".
  • Line-level and order-level discounts.
  • Taxes and duties notes based on your market settings.
  • Quantity limits, such as minimums, maximums and increments for B2B.

Discount codes

Shoppers enter discount codes at checkout. Automatic discounts show in the cart and on the checkout page.

Checkout buttons

The cart shows accelerated checkout buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) when they're turned on under Settings › Payments. Sienna doesn't change checkout itself. Customize checkout under Settings › Checkout.
