Sienna Beauty theme documentation

Sienna Beauty is a calm, editorial Shopify theme for skincare, beauty and wellness brands. It's built for routines, ingredients and storytelling, and it stays fast on any device.

Getting startedInstall the theme, add your logo, menus and products. Theme settingsColors, fonts, image frames, product cards, cart. SectionsEvery section, what it does and how to fill it in. Product pageBlocks, swatches, subscriptions, pickup and more. FAQQuick answers to the most common questions. SupportSend us a message and we reply within 2 business days.

What's included

  • Online Store 2.0: sections and blocks on every page, app blocks, and header and footer section groups.
  • Built for beauty: routine builder (add a whole routine to cart in one click), shoppable stories, ingredient spotlight, before and after slider, shop the look, video reviews and collection tabs.
  • Selling tools: quick add, cart drawer with order notes and automatic discounts shown, sticky add to cart, complementary and related products, subscriptions, gift card recipients, pickup availability and Shop Pay Installments.
  • Global: country and language selectors, right-to-left layouts (Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu) and currency codes.
  • Accessible and fast: keyboard and screen reader friendly, respects reduced motion, lazy-loaded responsive images, no jQuery.

Requirements

  • A Shopify store on any plan.
  • Optional free Shopify apps for some features: Search & Discovery (filters and complementary products) and a subscriptions app (subscriptions).
