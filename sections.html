Sections

You can add sections to the home page and to any page template. In the theme editor, click Add section. Most sections have a Color scheme setting, and many have a small label, heading, text and button.

Hero and banners

SectionUse it forTips
SlideshowThe opening hero with one or more slides.Choose Full width image or Image beside text. Image shape gives the arch look. Add a separate Mobile image for portrait crops. Auto-rotation includes a pause button.
Image bannerA single wide image with text.Use images at least 2400 px wide.
Campaign bannerA seasonal or launch message with an optional discount code.Choose an Occasion (for example Black Friday, Holiday season, Lunar New Year, Ramadan, Eid, Mother's Day or Diwali) to add a matching decorative motif. Create the discount code in Discounts first.
Sale countdownA countdown to a real end date.Enter the date as YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM in your store's time zone. The section hides itself (or shows your message) when the time is up. Use only real deadlines.

Products and collections

SectionUse it forTips
Featured collectionA grid or swipe row of products.Choose the collection, number of products and products per row. On mobile it can become a swipe row.
Collection tabsSeveral collections behind tabs, such as Bestsellers, New and Gifts.Add one Tab block per collection.
Collection listShop by type or by skin concern.Up to 12 collections. Each block uses the collection's image unless you choose a custom image or title.
Featured productSpotlight one product with buy buttons.Uses the same blocks as the product page.
Related products“You may also like” on product pages.Products come from Shopify's recommendations.
Shop the lookA lifestyle image with product hotspots.Add a Hotspot per product and set its horizontal and vertical position (%).

Beauty storytelling

SectionUse it forTips
StoriesInstagram-style round bubbles that open full-screen stories.Each Story has a bubble image, a full image or video, text and an optional product or link. Image duration sets how long each story shows.
Routine builder“Build your routine”: shoppers tick steps and add them all to the cart at once.Add a Step block per product (for example Cleanse, Treat, Moisturize) with a short note. The total updates live.
Ingredient spotlightHero ingredients with a benefit and a badge.Link each ingredient to a collection or page.
Before and afterA draggable comparison slider.Use two images with the same framing. Add a Disclaimer (for example “Individual results may vary”). Shoppers can drag it with a mouse, touch or arrow keys.
Video reviewsShort customer videos linked to products.Upload videos in Content › Files first.
TestimonialsWritten quotes with star ratings.Only use real customer reviews.
VideoA brand film from YouTube, Vimeo or an uploaded file.Add a cover image so the video only loads when played.

Text, trust and content

SectionUse it for
Image with textYour brand story next to an image.
Rich textA centered statement or short paragraph.
Icons with textPromises such as “Vegan”, “Cruelty-free” and “Recyclable”.
Logo listUp to 8 press or retailer logos, each with an optional link.
Collapsible contentFAQ. Add a Question block per question.
Blog postsLatest articles from a blog.
Email signupA newsletter form with optional image and legal text.
Contact formA form that sends messages to your store email.
Custom LiquidAdvanced: your own Liquid, HTML or app snippets.
AppsA container for app blocks from installed apps.

Apps

Sienna supports app blocks in the product information, featured product, cart, collection, search and blog post sections, as well as the Apps section. Install an app, then click Add block and pick it from the Apps list.
