Support

Need help with Sienna? Send us a message and we'll reply within 2 business days (Monday to Friday). The answer might already be in the FAQ.

What support covers: setting up and using the theme's features and settings, bugs in the theme, and help with theme updates.

Not covered: custom code changes, problems caused by edited theme code, and third-party apps. For app issues, contact the app developer.

If you send us your store URL, we may ask for collaborator access. You approve that request in your Shopify admin.

Sienna is a Shopify theme by codebyK. Can't find an answer? Contact support.
