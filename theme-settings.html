Theme settings

Theme settings apply to the whole store. In the theme editor, click the gear icon (Theme settings) in the sidebar.

Logo and favicon

SettingWhat it does
LogoShown in the header and on the password page.
Logo for dark backgroundsUsed when the header or footer uses a dark color scheme, such as Eucalyptus.
Logo widthDesktop logo width. Mobile scales it down automatically.
FaviconThe browser tab icon. Upload a square image of at least 32 × 32 px.

Colors

Sienna uses color schemes. Each scheme sets background, text, accent, button and border colors together, so every combination stays readable. The theme ships with five schemes:

SchemeDefault use
PorcelainPage background, most sections
BlushSoft highlight sections (testimonials, routine)
RoseCampaign and sale moments
EucalyptusAnnouncement bar and footer (dark)
SageCalm alternate sections

You can edit these or add more. Page color scheme sets the page background; Sale badge and Sold out badge color schemes set the badges.

For accessible text, keep strong contrast between each scheme's text and background. Aim for a ratio of at least 4.5:1, which you can check with any online contrast checker.

Typography

Choose heading and body fonts from the Shopify font library. They load from Shopify's servers, so they need no setup and stay fast. Font size scale makes headings or body text bigger or smaller. Use uppercase for small labels controls the small labels above headings.

Layout

SettingWhat it does
Page widthMaximum width of the content area.
Space between sectionsVertical rhythm of the page.
Image frameRounded (default), Arch (Sienna's signature arch top) or Square. Applies to cards and editorial images. The slideshow has its own Image shape setting.
CornersSoft, Subtle or Sharp corners on inputs, cards and panels.
Button shapePill or Match corners.
Show paper textureAdds a subtle paper grain to backgrounds.

Product cards

Choose the card style (Classic or Minimal), the image ratio (Portrait, Square or Adapt to image), and whether to show a second image on hover, the vendor, the product rating and a quick add button. Quick add opens a small window to choose a variant and add to cart without leaving the page.

Cart

Cart type: Drawer slides in from the side and keeps shoppers on the page. Page goes to the full cart page. Collection to show when cart is empty suggests products in an empty cart.

Search behavior

Turn on search suggestions to show products, collections, pages and articles as shoppers type. You can also show the vendor and price in the suggestions.

Currency format

Show currency codes shows prices as “$25.00 USD”. This is useful when you sell in several currencies.

Social media

Add your profile links. Icons appear in the footer's brand block.

Animations

Reveal sections on scroll fades sections in as they appear. Show hover effects adds gentle zoom and underline effects. Both are switched off automatically for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
